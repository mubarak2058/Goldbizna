10-Year Records Protection

The goal is to preserve business records continuously for at least 10 years, including sales, stock, customers, savings and payment-verification history. This vault creates dated archive files from records your signed-in account is allowed to read. It does not delete, move or edit live records.

How to make the 10-year promise real

1. Keep live records in Firestore.
Do not delete old sales, receipts, savings entries or customer history just to keep the POS fast. Use date filters and pagination for older records.
2. Create regular archives.
Export on a regular schedule and store copies in at least two separate places, such as a business computer and a secure cloud-drive account. Keep the original downloaded file unchanged.
3. Enable automated Google Cloud backups.
A project administrator must configure scheduled Firestore exports to a Cloud Storage bucket, then apply a 10-year object-retention policy or retention lock. This needs Google Cloud billing and administrator setup; a webpage alone cannot configure or guarantee it.
4. Test restoration.
At least quarterly, verify an archive can be opened and restored into a separate test project before relying on it.

Important protection notice

This is the first archive-control layer, not yet a verified automatic 10-year backup service. The repository's current Firestore rules permit reads only where explicitly granted; some collections may be blocked. We have deliberately not loosened those rules or changed your existing data. Automated scheduled exports and a locked 10-year Cloud Storage retention policy still need to be configured in Google Cloud.
